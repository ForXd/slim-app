# 0.7.5

Automatic theme remains month-based: February–April spring, May–July summer, August–October autumn, November–January winter. Manual choice takes priority; this is not exact solar-term timing.

Exercise distance in km / duration in minutes determines average speed for walking, running and cycling. The speed bands select approximate 2024 Adult Compendium values; other activities retain their preset. Empty distance falls back to the selected activity. Net energy subtracts resting expenditure. Legacy records retain their stored energy until parameters change or the user chooses recalculation. Direct energy input overrides the estimate.

Sources: https://pacompendium.com/bicycling/ , https://pacompendium.com/walking/ , https://pacompendium.com/running/ . Speed bands and terrain assumptions are estimates, not device measurements.
